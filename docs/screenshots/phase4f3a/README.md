have no Publish design, so it's drawn in their style. A Let's Play's Part Planner follows
in 4F-3b. The sidebar says 0.44.0: the pictures were taken just before the version changed.

**The data is made up** (a demo library): no real streams, names or folders. The frames
are drawn shapes standing in for game pictures, and the text and prompts are invented.

What differs from today's Publish, and why:

- **Each frame and its prompt sit on one card** (the creator's choice, 1 Nov): what's
  pasted into the Gemini app together is together. Today the frames and the prompts are
  in two separate lists.
- **Copy the picture is new** (the creator's choice): the frame goes on the clipboard and
  pastes straight into Gemini. Open the frame (Explorer) stays as the fallback.
- **Title ideas are a list to click**, the picked one marked with the accent bar.
- **A Let's Play's Publish follows the part clicked** in Parts or on the bar (the
  creator's choice), with Part buttons in the tab that pick the same part.
- **The episode number is kept with the plan**, so the Render panel and Publish show the
  same one; a guessed one says so in amber.
